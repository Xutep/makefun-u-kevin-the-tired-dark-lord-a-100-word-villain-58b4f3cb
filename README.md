A deterministic, stdlib-only Python program that writes a 100-word villain monologue to `/output/entry.txt`. The speaker is a dark lord who enchanted his own doorstep in the Year of the Black Frost, whose ravens and generals have all left him, and whose army of the Hollow March waits on one word from him at midnight. His tiredness lives in the pauses, the fragments and the small deflections — never in a plain statement that he is weary — and the last line lands its sting: he offers tea instead of the word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
